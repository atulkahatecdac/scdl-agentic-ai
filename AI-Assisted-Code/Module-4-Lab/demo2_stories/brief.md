# Business brief: College library - online book loans

*From: Head Librarian, to: IT team*

Students should be able to borrow books through the college portal instead of
queueing at the desk. A student can keep a book for two weeks. To be fair to
others, nobody should hold more than three books at a time. If a book comes back
late we charge five rupees a day, but the committee agreed a single book should
never cost a student more than a hundred rupees in fines. Students who owe fines
must clear them before borrowing again. Obviously, if all copies are out, the
book can't be borrowed.

We'd also like reminders before the due date - not urgent, maybe later.
