Customer Refund and Shipping Policy

Refunds
Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days of an approved return, credited to the
original payment method. Refund requests must be made within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be returned in their original, unused condition with all packaging intact.
A restocking fee of 10% applies to opened electronics unless the item is defective.

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs $5.99, or is free on orders over
$75. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99. Orders placed before
2 PM on a business day ship the same day; orders placed after 2 PM ship the next
business day.

Warranty
All products carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.
To make a warranty claim, contact support with the order number and a description
of the defect.
