Employee Leave Policy

Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited
at the start of each quarter. Unused annual leave can be carried forward into the
next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Leave requests must be submitted through the
HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for approval by the reporting manager.

Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate
is required for sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry
forward and resets at the start of each calendar year.

Maternity and Paternity Leave
Employees are eligible for 26 weeks of paid maternity leave and 2 weeks of paid
paternity leave, in line with statutory requirements. Leave must be applied for at
least 8 weeks before the expected date, where possible.

Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted their paid leave balance may request unpaid leave,
subject to manager approval. Extended unpaid leave beyond 30 days requires approval
from HR and the department head.
